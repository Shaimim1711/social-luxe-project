A few details

Before we
get started.

Who do you work with?

Brands and establishments looking to collaborate with talent or creators. Tell us about your business and the audience you want to reach.

What should I include in my brief?

Your brand or venue, the objective, your ideal timing, the audience and a working budget. An early idea is enough to start the conversation.

Can I enquire about a specific talent?

Yes. Include the person you have in mind in your brief. Availability, suitability and terms need to be discussed before a collaboration can be confirmed.
