Is this only for talent and public figures?

No. Luxe Concierge works with private clients as well as visiting talent. You do not need to be a creator or public figure to enquire.

Can you help outside Bali?

Tell us your destination and plans. We’ll discuss what can be arranged and confirm the scope before proceeding.

Does an enquiry confirm a reservation?

No. Availability, fees, pricing and booking terms must be agreed before any reservation is confirmed.
