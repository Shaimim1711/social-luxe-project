Good to know

Your questions.

Is the concierge only for talent and public figures?

No. Luxe Concierge by SL also works with private clients. You do not need to be a creator, celebrity or public figure to make an enquiry.

What information do you need?

Your destination or venue, preferred dates, number of guests, budget and any requests you would like us to consider.

Is my request a confirmed reservation?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. Availability, pricing and booking terms must be agreed before a reservation is confirmed.

Can I make a request that isn’t listed here?

Of course. Describe what you need and we’ll discuss whether we can help, including any fees and arrangements involved.
