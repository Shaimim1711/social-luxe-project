Privacy & enquiries.

Information you enter

The enquiry form prepares a message in your browser. Your form details are not uploaded to a site database. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the message from your own email app.

Email correspondence

If you email Social Luxe, the information you provide will be included in that correspondence. Share only what is needed for the initial enquiry. For privacy questions or requests about your correspondence, contact hello@socialluxe.com.au.

Site hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel, which may process connection information to serve and secure the site. This version does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

External links

Instagram and LinkedIn links open external services, which have their own privacy policies.
